# Google Home light scenes

Google doesn't let websites control Google Home lights directly. So the game uses voice:

- When an alarm goes off, the big screen says **"Hey Google, Rudolph mode"** (or that round's phrase) through the TV speakers.
- A Google speaker or Nest display near the TV hears it and runs a routine you set up ahead of time.
- When the alarm round ends, the screen says **"Hey Google, trivia mode"** to put the lights back.

Turn this on with the **"Big screen says 'Hey Google, …'"** switch on the host controller. That same section has a button for each phrase. Use them to test, or tap one if Google didn't hear it the first time. If Google ignores the TV's voice, just say the phrase yourself.

## Set up the routines (about 10 minutes)

On your phone, in the **Google Home** app:

1. Tap **Automations** → **+ Add** → **Household** (or **Personal**).
2. Under **Starters**, tap **Add starter** → **When I say to Google Assistant** and type the phrase from the table below.
3. Under **Actions**, tap **Add action** → **Adjust home devices**. Pick your lights and set the color and brightness.
4. Optional: add **Play and control media** or **Make Google Assistant say something** for an extra gag, like the speaker saying "Uh oh."
5. Save it, then repeat for each row.

| Phrase to set up | Round | Lights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `trivia mode` | Normal play | Warm white, about 70% |
| `Rudolph mode` | Rudolph Round | All red, 100% |
| `Grinch mode` | Grinch Round | All green, 100% |
| `Wet Bandits mode` | Wet Bandits Round | Half red, half blue (police lights) |
| `Griswold mode` | Griswold Round | Every light on, 100%, mixed colors |
| `Snow Day mode` | Snow Day Round | Cool white or icy blue |
| `Turbo mode` | Turbo Time | Orange or gold, 100% |
| `Nightmare mode` | Nightmare Round | Purple at 20%, or lights off |
| `Yippee mode` | Yippee-Ki-Yay Round | Orange, 100% |

## Test it before the party

1. Open the big screen and connect your phone as host.
2. Turn the voice switch on, then tap **Rudolph mode** in the lights section.
3. If the lights don't change: move the speaker closer to the TV, turn the TV up, or check the phrase in the Google Home app matches exactly.
